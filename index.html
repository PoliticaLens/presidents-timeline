All the content lives in data/presidents.json, data/wars.json, and data/events.json — edit dates, add positions, or add new shared moments there without touching script.js or this page. Presidential term and life dates are precise to the day; earlier career/military/office dates are mostly year-level approximations assembled from memory, not a live lookup — verify anything before publishing. This page loads its data with fetch(), so it needs to be served (GitHub Pages, or a local server) rather than opened directly as a file.
